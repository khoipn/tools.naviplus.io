Connection Check

Sometimes Navi+ will not open on one particular device or network. This page runs a deep test of how your connection reaches the Navi+ servers and the services the Navi+ app depends on (delay, lost packets, IPv6, packet sizes, proxies, browser storage…), so we can find the cause. It takes 1–2 minutes. Nothing is installed.
