Connection Check

Sometimes Navi+ will not open on one particular device or network. This page runs a deep test of how your connection reaches the Navi+ servers and the services the Navi+ app depends on (delay, lost packets, IPv6, packet sizes, proxies, browser storage…), so we can find the cause. It takes 1–2 minutes. Nothing is installed.

1Run the test

About 150 tiny requests plus a few downloads (in total ≈ 6 MB of data — please use Wi-Fi if you are on a limited mobile plan). Please keep this tab open until it finishes.

What was testedResultMedianProtocolServerDetails

2Watch for problems (best if the error comes and goes)

Leave this tab open (you can keep working in another tab). It quietly re-checks every 20 seconds and records exactly when and where a connection fails, and whether the rest of your internet was still working at that moment. Stops by itself after 3 hours. Your computer must stay awake.

3Send the results to Navi+ support

Preview exactly what will be sent 
Run the test first.

This includes your public IP address (needed to look up your connection in our logs) and technical details of your browser and network. It does not include passwords, store data, your exact location or anything from other tabs.
