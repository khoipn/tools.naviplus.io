Cap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Cap is a free Chrome extension by Navi+ Tools (tools.naviplus.io/toolset/images/cap) that lets you capture your current browser tab, add notes and highlights, and share the result with one link. This policy explains what Cap does with your data.

1. What Cap collects

  • Screenshot and annotations. Only when you click Publish, Cap uploads the screenshot of the tab you captured, together with the notes and highlights you added, to create a shareable page.
  • Page address. The URL and title of the captured tab may be included in the published page so viewers know what the screenshot shows.
  • Basic technical logs. Our servers and hosting provider may keep standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and timestamp) for security, abuse prevention and reliability.

Cap does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email or password, and we do not collect payment information.

2. What Cap does not do

  • It does not capture anything until you click the Cap icon.
  • It does not upload anything until you click Publish. Capturing and annotating happen in your browser.
  • It does not read your browsing history, passwords, cookies or form data.
  • It does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. How long we keep your data

Each published page is kept for 30 days and is then automatically and permanently deleted, together with the screenshot and notes it contains. After deletion the link stops working and we cannot recover the page.

Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

4. Who can see your published page

Anyone who has the link can view the page until it is deleted. Links are long and hard to guess, but they are not password protected. Do not capture or share passwords, payment details, personal data or confidential information. Only share the link with people you want to see it.

5. Service providers

Published pages are stored and delivered through trusted infrastructure providers (for example cloud storage and a content delivery network). They process data only to run the service for us and are not permitted to use it for other purposes.

6. Chrome permissions

Cap requests only the browser permissions it needs to capture the active tab when you click its icon and to publish the page you create. It does not use remote code and does not run in the background on the pages you visit.

7. Your choices

  • Don't click Publish and nothing leaves your browser.
  • Want a page removed before the 30 days end? Email us the link and we will delete it.
  • Uninstall Cap at any time from chrome://extensions.

8. Children

Cap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Cap handles data, we will update this page and the date above.
